    Primary sources: WAGN’s official fund page and daily FilePoint files · Yahoo Finance for quotes · SEC EDGAR for Dalal Street LLC’s manager-aggregated 13F and for WAGN’s own quarterly Form N-PORT, which is used only as a lagged cross-check of the daily file (its section says what was compared, as of when, and what could not be). Source status is shown in the page; a 13F is delayed and incomplete, and an N-PORT becomes public about 60 days after its quarter end. Times are shown in your local time zone (the zone is printed); on the public site every figure is as of the snapshot build time in the status line, and the page warns when that snapshot is more than 30 hours old.

    Changes are differences in the number of shares held between two official files. A holding whose share count did not change is not listed, even when a cash creation diluted its share per WAGN unit; a change that is merely proportional to a change in units outstanding (an in-kind basket) is not listed either. Where units changed, “vs pro-rata” restates the trade relative to deploying the flow in proportion to the previous portfolio. Neither number is proof of Pabrai’s exact trade time, execution price or motive.

    The holdings file is dated the next weekday and priced at the previous NAV date. Its pricing date is asserted only when NetAssets divided by the file’s own unit count equals the official NAV (within half a cent per unit); if units were created or redeemed after the NAV file, the page says so. Snapshots are saved whenever a new official file day is detected; if a file day is missed, only the net change over the displayed interval is knowable.
