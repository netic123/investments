Dates

upcoming · a configured entry stays listed until three days after its date; a filed shareholder report stays for 45 days after its filing date, and a due date appears up to 120 days ahead · the fund’s shareholder reports as SEC’s index lists them

    Primary sources: WAGN’s official fund page and daily FilePoint files · Yahoo Finance for quotes · SEC EDGAR for Dalal Street LLC’s manager-aggregated 13F and for WAGN’s own quarterly Form N-PORT, which is used only as a lagged cross-check of the daily file (its section says what was compared, as of when, and what could not be). Source status is shown in the page; a 13F is delayed and incomplete, and only the N-PORT for the last month of each fiscal quarter is public — it appears on EDGAR when the trust files it, which must be within 60 days of that quarter end.

    Changes are differences in the number of shares held between two official files. A holding whose share count did not change is not listed, even when a cash creation diluted its share per WAGN unit; a change that is merely proportional to a change in units outstanding (an in-kind basket) is not listed either. Cash-like rows (the money-market fund FXFXX, per the configuration) appear in the full table but are not listed as trades; a line under the table states their change. Where units changed, “vs pro-rata” restates the trade relative to deploying the flow in proportion to the previous portfolio. Neither number is proof of Pabrai’s exact trade time, execution price or motive.

    The holdings file is priced at the previous NAV date and dated the next NYSE trading day after it; it is published at about 00:02 UTC on the calendar day after the pricing date, Saturdays included (on Sat 5 Sept 2026 the file priced at the 4 Sept close was dated Tue 8 Sept, the trading day after Labor Day). Its pricing date is asserted only when NetAssets divided by the file’s own unit count equals the official NAV (within half a cent per unit); if units were created or redeemed after the NAV file, the page says so. The file’s NetAssets is that rounded NAV × its unit count — a pricing-date proof, not an independent asset total; the fund’s NAV file carries its own net assets figure, shown next to it. Files saved before 25 Aug 2026 report no unit count; a count shown for them is implied from NetAssets ÷ NAV and labelled so. Snapshots are saved whenever a new official file day is detected; if a file day is missed, only the net change over the displayed interval is knowable.
