    How to read this tab — sources, what counts as a trade, how the file is checked

    Primary sources: WAGN’s official fund page and daily FilePoint files · Yahoo Finance for quotes · SEC EDGAR for Dalal Street LLC’s manager-aggregated 13F and for WAGN’s own quarterly Form N-PORT, which is used only as a lagged cross-check of the daily file (its section says what was compared, as of when, and what could not be). Source status is shown in the page; a 13F is delayed and incomplete, and only the N-PORT for the last month of each fiscal quarter is public — it appears on EDGAR when the trust files it, which must be within 60 days of that quarter end.

    Changes are differences in the number of shares held between two official files; “Bought more”, “Reduced”, “New position” and “Sold out” name the direction of that difference, not a confirmed order. A holding whose share count did not change is not listed, even when a cash creation diluted its share per WAGN unit; a change that is merely proportional to a change in units outstanding (an in-kind basket) is not listed either. Cash-like rows (the money-market fund FXFXX, per the configuration) appear in the full table but are not listed as trades; a line under the table states their change. Where units changed, “vs pro-rata” (a column in the full change log, a tooltip in the portfolio table) restates the trade relative to deploying the flow in proportion to the previous portfolio. Neither number is proof of Pabrai’s exact trade time, execution price or motive. A trade is dated by the closes the two files are priced at: one session when the files are consecutive trading days, otherwise the range, and only the net change over that range is known.

    The holdings file is priced at the previous NAV date and dated the next NYSE trading day after it; it is published at about 00:02 UTC on the calendar day after the pricing date, Saturdays included (on Sat 5 Sept 2026 the file priced at the 4 Sept close was dated Tue 8 Sept, the trading day after Labor Day). Its pricing date is asserted only when NetAssets divided by the file’s own unit count equals the official NAV (within half a cent per unit); if the file carries more or fewer units than the NAV file of that date (a creation or redemption booked after that NAV was struck), the page says so. The file’s NetAssets is that rounded NAV × its unit count — a pricing-date proof, not an independent asset total; the fund’s NAV file carries its own net assets figure, shown next to it. Files saved before 25 Aug 2026 report no unit count; a count shown for them is implied from NetAssets ÷ NAV and labelled so. Snapshots are saved whenever a new official file day is detected; if a file day is missed, only the net change over the displayed interval is knowable.

    About this page · sources · privacy · not investment advice

    About this page. An independent, unofficial dashboard published by the repository owner; it is not affiliated with Pabrai Wagons ETF, Dalal Street LLC, Yahoo or the SEC. Source code and every build: github.com/netic123/investments. — Raw inputs: fund holdings CSV · daily NAV CSV · this page’s holdings JSON. Times are shown in your local time zone, each with the zone abbreviation that applied at that instant. On the public site every figure is as of the snapshot build time in the status line, which also shows the snapshot’s age.

    Not investment advice. What your browser contacts: this site’s own JSON files (on every page load and on the 10-minute re-check); Google Fonts for the typefaces; data.sec.gov (SEC’s EDGAR submissions index), on a page load and, at most once an hour, on a re-check that loads a newer snapshot, while the published snapshot carries the manually verified 13F fallback — whichever tab you have open — to check whether a newer filing exists; api.github.com, only when you open Build history (an unauthenticated read of the last 20 runs) or when the owner’s live update runs with a stored token, which it does on the Update button and, if the owner switched on automatic rebuilds, by itself on load, when the tab becomes visible again, and on the 10-minute re-check. Nothing you type on this page is sent anywhere, except the owner’s token and rebuild interval, which the live update sends to api.github.com — the interval is also published in the build’s reason.
